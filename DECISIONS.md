# Current playtest rulings
The live design and component sheet own rules and data. These active TEST rulings clarify implementation.

1. Team mode uses four heroes, 20 PP and three free Recruits per faction, with shared control and hand.
2. Both factions share Fire, Water, Earth and Wind. Use a common three-element set for normal play and four for team mode; independently shuffle assignments after roster lock. The shared deck has nine cards per active element.
3. Starting hand is 2 tactics; each objective awards 1 VP + 1 tactic. Recruits have 1 HP, Move 3, Attack 2 and Defense 2.
4. Both queues use every position. The final 5 AP / 2 RAP entitlement is intentional. Snapshot entitlement before shifting.
5. Scenario scoring precedes Poison at own-turn start. Resolve immediate victory at each award. Record enemy source for Poison/forced-hazard defeat scoring and apply settlement once.
6. Later-scenario modifiers resolve from persisted setup values and simultaneous source snapshots. Snow affects ordinary Move, Heat adds its extra once, and Wind is an optional free step.
7. Hero Notes own targeting and duration details. Granted movement preserves paid counters. Demea uses a live active aura; Tavi expires this turn; Perrin reads his Enraged stack.
8. Cancel targets the immediately preceding opponent combat tactic, including Cancel. A boost advances that opportunity. Ability payments resolve directly. Counterattack grants one optional retaliation.
9. Snapshot statuses at the roll boundary; preserve newly gained after-effects. Apply Light Attack cap after damage additions.
10. Objective attack conditions evaluate at the dice boundary while held/eligible. Break the Levy tracks distinct Recruit IDs that turn. Claim the Starfall checks every selected hero on an X.
11. Current TEST settlement is 10 RP base, +1 victory and +2 per secured stone. Component prices and regional availability follow the live sheet.
12. Private projections govern all UI and bot decisions. Revealed cards create the existing undo barrier; public events supply card-history and reveal presentation.
13. Phalexia is the formation region’s name. Current playable heroes are the ten complete starters.
