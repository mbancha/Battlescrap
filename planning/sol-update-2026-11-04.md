1. Confirmed rules and current local changes
Designer confirmed 4 November: start each faction with 2 tactics, hand limit 8; objectives award 1 VP + 1 tactic. These replace older 1/4-card starting hands and 3-tactic objective rewards. Moonstone pickup still gives its collecting hero 1 tactic + 1 Focus and secures 2 RP at settlement. Hero defeat drops a Moonstone, draws one tactic for that hero's owner and moves its queue card to slot 0. Recruits receive none of those benefits and cannot collect stones manually or automatically.
Keep the single shared draw/discard supply, private hands and 27/36 total cards. Each active element has 2 Attack, 2 damage and one each Defense, Cancel, counterattack, Move/heal and Recruit assault. Fire, Water, Earth and Wind are shared by both factions; independently assign the same active set after roster lock. Three heroes omit one common element; four heroes use all four.
Normal setup: 15 PP, three heroes and three free Recruits per faction. Shared-faction 3-4 player TEST setup: 20 PP, four heroes, three Recruits; partners share a hand/control and alternate by faction. Recruits remain HP2 / Move3 / Attack2 / Defense2. Existing equipment and Recruit upgrades remain; tactic upgrades and faction decks remain removed.
Queue, bottom to top: two-player 1/1, 2/1, 3/1, 3/2, 4/3, 5/2 AP/RAP; 3-4-player 1/1, 2/1, 2/2, 3/1, 3/2, 4/2, 4/3, 5/2. The final RAP decrease is intentional. Every slot including 0 is selectable; no queue tactic draw. Snapshot entitlement before shifting. AP pays for active hero or friendly Recruits; RAP pays only for Recruits, spent first. Keep independent hero action counters, one shared paid-action counter per Recruit, separate paid-ability counters and no counter cost for granted movement/free assault.
Keep own-turn scenario scoring and immediate victory, then Poison 1 damage/remove 1, then activation selection. Healing restores HP and clears all Poison. Preserve last-HP Enraged, status roll snapshots, exact-range restrictions, ranged -2 engagement and -2 extension penalties, zero-pool two-dice/keep-lower, hit damage per kept six and winning-defense Focus per kept six. End-turn pickups, overflow, one objective, finite objective refill, authored end effects, fire and modifier timing must remain resumable.
Scenario configuration now supports tacticsPerVP and unitInZone victory with an explicit unit ID and destination hexes. Do not infer these from map art; absent configuration keeps the selected VP target. Three Fires remains north +1 VP and south +1 per controlled bonfire at own-turn start, +1 per enemy defeat, first to 15. Preserve six later-scenario modifiers and seeded setup; campaign base income and team mode remain TEST, unfinished regional recruitment/travel/catch-up formula OPEN.
2. Combat decisions and truthful logs
The 2 November combat timing supersedes the old general-purpose Cancel stack instructions. Hero ability payments and out-of-combat tactics resolve without prompting the inactive faction. During an attack the defender still gets an ordinary combat-card opportunity even when the attacker played no card. After either combatant plays a tactic, offer the opponent legal boosts, Pass and Cancel only against the immediately preceding opponent tactic. A boost forgoes cancellation of the earlier tactic. Preserve Cancel-on-Cancel, existing pass/close timing and spent-card costs. Automatically advance if Pass is the only legal option; do not reveal hidden hands.
Keep a chronological Gameplay Log from bidding through settlement, with every committed human/bot action, payments, targets, voluntary passes, rolls, damage and automatic consequences grouped with their triggering action. Attribute faction and unit, keep draws anonymous, and derive exports from the same ordered entries. Undo removes the reverted branch and save/resume retains the sequence.
Show the current action and combat decision above the readable scrolling log. All legal battle cards must remain selectable there. Human rolls use the existing mouse/touch swipe handle with a keyboard equivalent; show the short animated dice sequence. Gesture strength never changes results, bots roll automatically, and resolution advances automatically when no reroll or other legal choice remains.
