1. Rules and data contract

Normal setup uses 15 PP, three distinct available heroes and three free Recruits per faction. Shared-faction 3–4-player TEST setup uses 20 PP, four heroes and three Recruits; partners share faction control/resources and alternate by faction.

Recruits have exactly 1 HP, Move 3, Attack 2 and Defense 2 before upgrades. Their defeat, Poison, healing, respawn, bot evaluation, inspector, card face, help text and tests must derive from that definition. A single point of Poison damage defeats a full-health Recruit.

Both factions use the same three active elements in two-player mode and all four in team mode, independently shuffled onto their selected heroes after roster lock. One shared tactic draw/discard supply serves private hands. Each element contributes nine cards: two Attack +2, two hit-damage +2, and one each Defense +2, Cancel, counterattack, Move4/heal1 and Recruit assault. Total 27 or 36. Start with 2 tactics per faction; hand limit 8.

Each objective awards 1 VP and 1 tactic. One hidden starting objective, hand limit 2, next-own-turn eligibility, at most one claim at friendly turn end, and an empty-hand draw from the finite six-card supply. Attack conditions evaluate at the dice boundary; end-position conditions evaluate at the claim checkpoint. Claim the Starfall requires all selected heroes on printed X locations: three normally or four in team mode.

Hero Moonstone pickup grants that hero 1 Focus, draws 1 tactic and secures 2 RP for settlement. Hero defeat drops one stone, draws 1 tactic for its owner and moves its queue entry to bottom. Recruit defeat resolves scenario scoring. Moonstone collection belongs to heroes.

Queue bottom to top, AP/RAP: six positions = 1/1, 2/1, 3/1, 3/2, 4/3, 5/2; eight positions = 1/1, 2/1, 2/2, 3/1, 3/2, 4/2, 4/3, 5/2. Every position is selectable. Snapshot entitlement before shifting. RAP pays Recruit actions first, then AP; AP also pays the active hero.

Each hero basic action Move/Attack/Grab/Respawn has an independent escalating counter, 1/2/3 AP. Each Recruit shares one paid-action counter across actions, 1/2/3 points. Paid abilities have their own repeat cost from printed base. Granted movement, swaps, displacement and free assault preserve paid-action counters. Respawn preserves counters.

Turn start: scenario scoring and immediate victory check, then each friendly poisoned unit takes 1 damage and removes 1 Poison, then activation selection. Healing restores HP up to maximum and clears Poison. Damage from above 1 HP to exactly 1 grants a hero 1 Enraged each time.

Combat compares highest die, then six counts when both maxima are six, with remaining ties won by attacker. Hit damage is 1 + each kept attacker six + bonuses, with Light Attack cap last. Successful defense grants Focus per kept defender six. Zero pool rolls two and keeps the lower. Snapshot participating statuses at the roll boundary; newly gained after-effects survive.

Ranged engagement is −2 Attack dice; extended ordinary range up to double printed range is −2 more. Exact/fixed ranges follow their explicit restriction. Melee flanking counts eligible other allies. Preserve authored Push/Pull, Fly, terrain-entry, fire-end and targeting boundaries.

At activation end, resolve hero pickups/overflow, one objective claim/reward, empty objective hand draw, authored end effects, fire and scenario end effects in their defined order. Victory ends the sequence immediately. Defeat, settlement and reward consequences apply once.

2. Combat responses and ongoing effects

During an attack, offer the defender its ordinary combat-card opportunity even if the attacker played no card. Alternate legal tactics and passes through the response window. Cancel targets the opponent’s immediately preceding combat tactic, including Cancel; playing a boost advances the opportunity to the newly played tactic. Preserve spent cards and committed costs.

Hero ability payments and out-of-combat tactics resolve directly. Automatically advance forced Pass and resolve a roll when all meaningful reroll/response choices are complete. An optional counterattack is one legal retaliation against the original attacker.

Represent ongoing abilities as explicit active state and duration. Demea’s live aura grants other adjacent allies Heavy Attack (2) while active, expires at next activation start or defeat, and has one active instance. Tavi lasts through the current friendly turn; Perrin’s passive reads his current Enraged stack. Update the existing ongoing-state representation, UI and save schema together.

Keep multi-target movement, attack sequences, optional steps, private overflow and responses serializable and resumable. Project only authorized information into player and bot views.

9. Simulation reporting

Continue running multiple different legal team rosters across paired seeds, swapping seats/deployment assignments for comparison. Report hero-level results aggregated across those varied teams. Retain roster/seed/config metadata in raw results for reproducibility. The summary unit is the individual hero.

For each hero show appearances, wins and win rate: a win is a victory by the faction fielding that hero, and the denominator is completed games in which that hero was fielded. Include sample counts and a confidence interval, balance appearances where practical, and label low-sample results. These are roster-associated outcomes under the tested bot policy, rather than an isolated measure of hero strength. Separate three-hero and four-hero modes and materially different scenarios/configurations.

For completed games report these per-game averages, counting both factions together:

• Total gameplay actions: count each resolved paid basic action, paid unique ability activation and printed tactic play once. Record granted/free moves and attacks separately; report their total alongside paid actions and tactic plays. Setup inputs, passes, target selection, dice clicks, response housekeeping and automatic consequences are separate telemetry events. This definition keeps UI implementation details from inflating the gameplay-action count.

• Game length in faction turns: one faction activation is one turn; include a terminal turn that ends at its start-of-turn scoring checkpoint. Label the unit explicitly.

• Hero deaths and Recruit deaths, separately, plus combined deaths. Count each defeat event, including repeated deaths after respawn, once.

• Tactic cards earned per game, with totals by source: Moonstone pickup, hero-defeat compensation, tactical objectives, scenario VP rewards, hero/regional abilities, and any other explicitly authored draw source. Record starting hands and purchased starting draws separately from earned cards. Count cards actually received; also retain requested draws, supply shortages and overflow discards so the totals can be reconciled. Pass an explicit source identifier through the draw pipeline.

Recommended supporting measures: median and 10th/90th percentiles for game length and action count; first-player/deployment-side win rates; VP earned by source; stalled/turn-limit games; and hero activation counts. These help distinguish queue access, scoring and bot-flow problems from hero strength. Show completed/attempted game counts and excluded runs alongside averages.

Implement counters at authoritative committed engine events and aggregate in the simulation runner. Keep telemetry deterministic and preserve it through replay/save as appropriate. Include seed, rules/content version, bot-policy version, mode and scenario in exports. The UI presents a compact game-summary panel, a hero-results table and a tactic-income breakdown.

Acceptance: varied rosters still run; paired seeds are reproducible; hero appearances/wins reconcile with match outcomes; per-source tactic receipts sum to earned totals; setup draws stay separate; repeated hero/Recruit defeats count correctly; free effects and multi-step prompts follow the action-count definition; early victory, stalled runs and zero completed-game batches produce clear results. 6 November 2026 — Shields and class keywords (active TEST)
Hero raw cost = Move + Defense + Attack + printed Range + HP + 3 per Shield + unique ability points + class keyword points. Fly costs 1 raw point; basic Heavy Attack (1) costs 2 raw points. Final PP = ceiling(raw / 3), rounded once. Primary Guards gain 1 Shield; primary Scouts gain Fly; primary Strikers gain Heavy Attack (1) on basic attacks only. Hero HP is reduced by 1, minimum 2. Shields reduce attack damage by 1 each, minimum zero; apply Light Attack caps after reductions. Shields do not prevent terrain, Poison or other non-attack damage. Pierce ignores Shields and discards the defending Fortified stack without its Defense bonus at the roll boundary, hit or miss. Fortify in this instruction refers to the existing Fortified status. No current hero automatically receives Pierce. Fly ignores intervening units and terrain during voluntary movement, requires a legal empty destination and resolves landing hazards; it does not alter forced movement. Hit damage = 1 + max(0, kept attacker sixes − 1) + Enraged + Heavy Attack + declared bonuses, less Shields unless Pierce. First six adds no damage. Defender Focus is unchanged. Revised preparation budgets: 21 PP for three heroes, 28 PP for four heroes (TEST).
