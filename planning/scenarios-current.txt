Moonfall Tactics — Scenarios

Three Fires

Each scenario uses a map plus its own deployment/spawning edges, special rules, VP sources and victory threshold or explicit VP payout. A map can support multiple scenarios and multiple group spawning edges; scenario rules specify which are active. Scenario configuration defines victory.

Three Fires: water/north scores 1 VP at the start of each of its own turns, regardless of bonfire control. Stone/south scores 1 VP PER bonfire it controls at the start of each of its own turns. Control means more adjacent friendly units than enemy units; ties are uncontrolled. First to 15 VP wins; check immediately after every award. 1 VP per enemy defeat and no friendly-fire VP.

Random setup: every map has nine X locations. For this map shuffle four traps and five Moonstone tokens randomly onto the Xs, all visible before play.

Any movement into a trap deals 1 damage, then removes it. Any movement into fire deals 1 damage; ending a character’s turn there also deals 1 damage. Resolve each entry separately, including voluntary and forced movement, stopping if defeated. Fire hexes are enterable. TEST recruit timing: fire end-turn damage applies to recruits that acted during that friendly turn. Moving an idle hero by an ability is not itself ending its activation.

Deployment: use the chosen north blue or south stone zone. Every full hex in that zone is eligible if otherwise legal; a few pixels of map alignment error are acceptable. Half hexes are never eligible. If the zone is full, use the nearest legal empty overflow hex.

Battlefield modifiers

After the first scenario, roll a d6 once at scenario setup, after revealing traps and Moonstones. The result lasts for that scenario. Modifier rules appear in the scenario reference.

1 Plague: at faction turn end, each living unit adjacent to at least one poisoned living unit gains 1 Poison. Snapshot sources simultaneously. TEST timing.

2 Wind: roll one fixed axial direction at setup. Each friendly unit may take one optional free {hex:1} step that direction per own turn; normal occupancy and entry hazards apply.

3 Storm: ranged attacks have −1 Attack die.

4 Snow: ordinary Move allowance is reduced by {hex:1}, minimum zero; fixed ability/tactic movement is unchanged (TEST).

5 Heat: gaining Enraged from zero adds exactly 1 extra; add the extra once.

6 Moonfall: at setup add one Moonstone to a random legal full adjacent hex per trap; stones may stack.
