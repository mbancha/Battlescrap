Moonfall Tactics — Rules Quick Sheet
shared-elements-v15 design update · 30 October 2026 · TEST defaults are adjustable. Full rules and lore are in the main Design Draft tab; hero-specific wording is in the live Moonfall Tactics sheet.
Setup and turn
Prepare: 15 PP; choose three heroes and three free Recruits. Team 4v4 TEST: 20 PP and four heroes per faction. Bid secretly for deployment; higher bid wins a contested zone/first turn and compensates the opponent. Only unused compensation survives as attacker reroll PP.
Fire (red flame), Water (turquoise droplet), Earth (brown rock), and Wind (sky-blue gust). Both factions use the same elements. Display element color/symbol together with faction color/insignia as a two-color identity code. For 1v1, choose one element to omit for both sides and use the same three active elements; four-hero games use all four. After roster lock, independently shuffle the active elements with state RNG and assign one to each hero on each side. Players cannot choose or swap assignments. Match map piece, queue holder and hero card.
Deploy: living models occupy full empty hexes in their zone or nearest legal overflow. Both factions share one 27-card deck (36 with all four elements); draw 2 tactics, limit 8. Draw one hidden objective, limit 2.
Turn start: scenario VP, immediate victory, then each friendly poisoned unit takes 1 damage/removes 1 Poison. Choose any friendly hero, capture AP/RAP, shift its queue card to bottom; remove its ongoing ability tokens.
Queue AP/RAP bottom→top: 3v3 — 1/1, 2/1, 3/1, 3/2, 4/3, 5/2. 4v4 TEST — 1/1, 2/1, 2/2, 3/1, 3/2, 4/2, 4/3, 5/2. No queue draws.
Actions: heroes may Move, Attack, Grab or Respawn; Recruits may Move, Attack or Respawn. AP pays for active hero or friendly Recruits; RAP only Recruits (spend first). Each hero action independently costs 1/2/3…; each Recruit shares one 1/2/3… counter. Respawn fully heals/clears statuses, but never resets costs. Ability/tactic movement costs no extra action.
Abilities: discard a tactic matching the hero’s assigned element, plus printed AP. Generic rainbow ? means that element. Card-only repeats need another card, no AP. Ongoing effects require an ability token after payment; expire at next own activation start or defeat.
Combat and statuses
Roll: defender/attacker alternate tactics or pass; two passes roll. Compare highest face, then six counts only if both highest are 6; attacker wins remaining ties. Zero pool rolls two D6 and keeps only lower. Attacker rerolls own dice for 1 PP each from bid compensation; resolve automatically when no reroll or card play is legal; otherwise finish explicitly.
Hit: 1 damage + each kept attacker six + Enraged + Heavy Attack(x) + declared damage bonuses, capped last by Light Attack(x). Winning defense gains one Focus per kept six. No reward from losing-side sixes.
Modifiers: melee +1 Attack die per other adjacent ally with no other adjacent enemy. Ranged −2 if any enemy adjacent, and −2 beyond range to double range; stack both. Exact ranges never extend. Nothing blocks sight.
Focus / Enraged: next attack gets +1 die / +1 hit damage per token; spend complete participating stacks after attack, even on miss. Fortified / Dazed: +1 / −1 Defense die per token, spend both participating stacks after defense. New after-effect tokens survive.
Poison: on-hit Poison(x) adds x; own-turn tick deals 1/removes 1. Heal restores HP AND clears all Poison, even at full HP. Hero damage from above 1 HP to exactly 1 gives 1 Enraged, each time, including after healing.
Movement keywords: follow the scenario’s terrain rules. Push/Pull moves strictly away/toward fixed source by one each step, obeying occupancy/full hexes and hazards, stopping on defeat. Fly ignores intervening units/terrain, destination still legal/hazardous.
Tactics, rewards and victory
Card use: printed tactic effect OR matching-element ability payment. During combat, Cancel answers only the opponent’s immediately preceding tactic and can cancel Cancel. Playing another boost gives up the earlier cancellation opportunity. Ability payments and out-of-combat tactics do not interrupt the inactive player. Paid cards stay spent. Counterattack triggers on a miss, ordinary range/responses, optional, no chains.
Deck: each element has two +2 Attack, two +2 hit damage, one Cancel, +2 Defense, counterattack-on-miss, Move {hex:4}/heal 1 and Recruit assault. Printed effects require no element match. Reshuffle the shared discard; no automatic empty-hand draw.
End: automatically collect stones beneath living friendly heroes (never Recruits) and resolve overflow; claim at most one eligible objective for 1 VP + 1 tactic; draw an objective only if hand empty/deck nonempty; resolve authored end effect/fire; apply scenario end-turn effects; pass. Immediately stop if any VP award reaches victory.
Objectives: follow the condition printed on your card. Only held, eligible objectives count; eligibility begins on your next own turn. Claim at most one at turn end for 1 VP + 1 tactic. The finite six-card objective deck never reshuffles.
Moonstone: only heroes collect; Recruits cannot collect by any method. Each pickup draws one tactic and grants the collecting hero 1 Focus; each secured stone pays 2 RP at settlement. Hero defeat drops one stone, draws one tactic for owner and moves its card to bottom. Recruit defeat does neither. Secured stones never drop again.
